Types and Topology

A workshop in honour of
Martín Escardó's 60th birthday

17 December 2025

About

Martín Escardó, professor in Theoretical Computer Science at the University of Birmingham, will turn 60 on 22 November 2025. We are organizing a workshop to celebrate this occasion as well as Martín's diverse contributions to constructive mathematics, domain theory, locale theory, logic, topology and homotopy/univalent type theory.

The workshop will take place in Birmingham on 17 December 2025.
